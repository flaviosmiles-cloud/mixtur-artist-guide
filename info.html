Getting around Barcelona

Metro & Bus

Recommended transport options during your stay

Recommended · Occasional use

T-casual · 1 zone

10 journeys · €13.00

A good option if you only expect to use public transport a few times during the festival.

Recommended · Frequent use

T-usual · 1 zone

Unlimited travel for 30 days · €22.80

Best option if you expect to travel frequently. Personal and non-transferable.

Important

Airport

T-usual is valid at Aeroport T1 and T2 metro stations. T-casual is not valid for the airport metro stations.

Where to buy

Metro ticket machines

Tickets can be purchased at metro stations. Follow the instructions for T-mobilitat.

Getting around Barcelona

Taxi

Official taxis are black and yellow

Official option

AMB Mobilitat · Picmi

Request the nearest metropolitan taxi from your phone.

App

FREENOW

Book and pay for Barcelona taxis through the app.

Practical

Street taxis

Available taxis can also be hailed directly on the street or found at official taxi ranks.
